01 / ABOUT

Build. Learn. Connect.

Event Overview

The SEU Tech Event is designed to give students a practical introduction to current technology topics. Participants can attend focused sessions, explore real examples, and connect classroom concepts with practical projects.

The main goal is to encourage students to learn by building, asking questions, and collaborating with peers.
